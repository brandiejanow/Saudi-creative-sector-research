Timeline

The research began in 2019. This timeline will document change year by year as the supporting records are verified.

How the timeline will develop

Each year needs its own evidence.

Later entries will separate events that happened, changes documented in the sector, research conducted that year, and retrospective interpretation. When a record is undated or uncertain, the uncertainty will remain visible rather than assigning a year without evidence.
