Chronology / Source status / 2019 to 2026

Timeline

A year-by-year account of what the available material can document, with the distinction between recorded discussions, later interpretations and verified public developments kept visible.

Research began in 2019   |   Eight years of source coverage   |   Review in progress

01 / A changing archive

The record is not evenly distributed.

The source inventory includes records dated across 2019 to 2026. Their number, purpose, and institutional context vary by year. Later periods contain more recorded activity, but a more extensive file archive does not mean that the national sector was more active or that each source represents a separate study.

An initial chronological summary appears below. The descriptions identify what is currently indexed; they are not a substitute for completed review of the underlying private original sources.

02 / Dated source coverage

What the documents show so far.

2019

Initial research record

The working source register identifies dated primary qualitative research beginning in March 2019 about sector definition, production capacity, knowledge transfer, education routes and related institutional questions.

Evidence status: Original qualitative research held privately; source details and names are not published. [A01]

2020

Support and production routes

The private original qualitative research from 2020 addresses manufacturing, contracts, distribution and barriers at different career stages. Individual identities and source occasions are not part of this public version.

Evidence status: Private original research; source context retained confidentially. [A01]

2021

From questions to proposed responses

Early records concern connecting skills to professions, work experience, mentorship, local production, and ways to make specialist resources discoverable.

Evidence status: Working research chronology; full qualitative coding remains in progress. [A01]

2022

Operating the support system

Several working records address membership structure, access fees, service delivery, mentorship matching, and the cost of sustaining shared resources. These are discussions and plans, not proof that proposed services succeeded.

Evidence status: Dated planning records; source-by-source comparison pending. [A01]

2023

Professional experience and perceived value

The archive contains a qualitative research report and related original qualitative records concerning education, clients, career access, and professional value. An inconsistency between the report's stated study date and one private source date remains unresolved.

Evidence status: Date discrepancy retained in the private source audit; no identifying quotations published. [A01]

2024

Institutional and service structures

The archive becomes denser in the second half of 2024 and records discussions of membership, shared services, professional infrastructure, labs, and research design. The Ministry also records a changing cultural sector in its 2024 report.

Evidence status: Private organizational records and separately published institutional statistics. [A01]

2025

Authored funding research proposals

Two 2025 research briefs prepared under the author's name document questions about financial infrastructure, legal status, production resources, private capital, and unequal access. A related submission proposed interviews, workshops, surveys, and a national funding map.

Evidence status: Unpublished drafts; proposals are not completed outcomes. [P01]

2026

Archive review and public research edition

The multi-year inventory is being reconciled against sources, dates, duplicate files, and publication rights. Official statistics and financing reports are being introduced as outside points of comparison. The public site is established in 2026.

Evidence status: Public website and research source-control update; ongoing review. [A01]

03 / Questions across time

Continuity is a claim to test, not assume.

A concern recorded in 2019 and one expressed in 2025 may use similar language while describing different conditions. Research must compare the participants, sector, region, institutions and available alternatives before calling a problem unchanged.

For example, the existence of a 2024 financing program is a documented change in the institutional landscape. Whether the 2025 concerns about access persisted for particular groups remains a separate research question.

Future versions will link each historical entry to a source identifier, exact date, anonymized extract where permitted, and evidence of any later response or outcome.
