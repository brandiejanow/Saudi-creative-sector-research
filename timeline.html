01 / A changing archive

Chronological scope

The chronology summarizes dated original research from 2019 through 2026. Research coverage varies by year; the entries document subjects investigated rather than measuring total sector activity.

02 / Dated source coverage

Historical research summary

2019

Initial research record

The original research record begins in March 2019 about sector definition, production capacity, knowledge transfer, education routes and related institutional questions.

2020

Support and production routes

The 2020 material addresses manufacturing, contracts, distribution and barriers at different career stages.

2021

From questions to proposed responses

Early records concern connecting skills to professions, work experience, mentorship, local production, and ways to make specialist resources discoverable.

2022

Operating the support system

Several working records address membership structure, access fees, service delivery, mentorship matching, and the cost of sustaining shared resources. These are discussions and plans, not proof that proposed services succeeded.

2023

Professional experience and perceived value

The archive contains a qualitative research report and related original qualitative records concerning education, clients, career access, and professional value. An inconsistency between the report's stated study date and one private source date remains unresolved.

2024

Institutional and service structures

Research in 2024 addressed membership, shared services, professional infrastructure, technical facilities, and research design. The Ministry also records a changing cultural sector in its 2024 report.

2025

Authored funding research proposals

Two research briefs developed in 2025 document questions about financial infrastructure, legal status, production resources, private capital, and unequal access. A related submission proposed interviews, workshops, surveys, and a national funding map.

2026

Archive review and public research edition

The public research edition was established in 2026, bringing original historical material into comparison with published statistics and financing reports.
