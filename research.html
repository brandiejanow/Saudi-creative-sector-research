Saudi Creative Sector Research / Began in 2019

Research

The people, institutions and conditions behind changes in Saudi Arabia's design industry and creative sector.

Independent research by Brandie Janow / Source review underway

Areas of study

Beyond the finished project.

The research follows the conditions in which creative activity takes place, not only the names of projects or the institutions associated with them. The areas below are the scope of the research, not claims that all related findings have been verified.

01

Education and entry

Access to education, skills, early employment and routes into design and creative professions.

02

Professional conditions

Employment, independent practice, professional recognition and the experiences of creative workers.

03

Funding and market access

Support for businesses and practitioners, payment, project opportunities and barriers to participation.

04

Commissioning and procurement

How projects are commissioned, who can compete, and how selection and purchasing decisions affect the sector.

05

Institutions and development

The changing roles of government bodies, cultural institutions, professional organizations and educational systems.

06

Production and labor

The full range of people involved in producing design, alongside manufacturing, construction and conditions of labor.

Publication status

Source review before publication.

Existing documents, field observations and research records from 2019 onward are being checked for dates, authorship, permissions and appropriate use. Completed studies will be added here with their evidence and limitations.

Research produced under separate client or organizational agreements, including Rukun's Saudi Creative Index, is not presented as independent research by Brandie Janow.

Saudi Creative Sector Research is independent research begun in 2019 by Brandie Janow. This public record is maintained on GitHub, with source review and publication continuing.
