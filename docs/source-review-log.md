## Known disagreement

Two related private research records from 2023 contain differing dates. The public timeline does not select one without further checking.

A financing program existed in 2024. A 2025 proposal nevertheless raised questions about accessibility. These statements address different issues. An existing program is not proof of universal access, while a research concern is not proof that all funding routes failed.

The original research archive includes some other materials classified outside the present study. Its overall file count is not used as a count of unique research participants or completed national surveys.

## Next source review

Review 2019 to 2021 private qualitative research sources for date and attribution, reconcile 2023 dates, compare 2024 to 2026 public program measures with participant-level evidence where permitted, and document counterexamples as well as supporting material.
