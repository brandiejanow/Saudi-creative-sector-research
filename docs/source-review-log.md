## Known disagreement

A retrospective 2023 research report and a source transcript have different study dates. The public timeline does not select one without further checking.

A financing program existed in 2024. A 2025 proposal nevertheless raised questions about accessibility. These statements address different issues. An existing program is not proof of universal access, while a research concern is not proof that all funding routes failed.

The private archival register includes non-research content. Its total recording count is not being used as the number of research interviews or studies.

## Next source review

Review 2019 to 2021 recordings for date and authorship, reconcile 2023 dates, compare 2024 to 2026 public program measures with participant-level evidence where permitted, and document counterexamples as well as supporting material.
