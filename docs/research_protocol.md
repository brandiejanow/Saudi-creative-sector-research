# Research protocol

Saudi Creative Sector Research  
Brandie Janow  
Research begun 2019; public repository established in 2026.

## Research purpose

Examine the development of Saudi Arabia's design industry and creative sector through dated original documents, field observations, qualitative accounts, institutional material, and public measures. Distinguish the number of programs and projects from the conditions affecting professional access and continuity.

## Corpus boundary

The working historical archive contains mixed material from different organizations and periods. Some recordings are unrelated to research; many remain uncoded; some belong to restricted projects. No raw counts are presented as independent interviews or national study samples until relevance and permissions have been reviewed.

Do not import Rukun Saudi Creative Index research into this independent project. The user's historic authored grant briefs can support a history of proposed questions, subject to attribution and publication rights; they are not evidence that the proposed study was completed.

## Public release conditions

Every public claim requires source identifier, date, document type, setting, relevant population and interpretation limit. For direct quotations, also require original-language verification, accurate transcript and rights or appropriate consent. Redact personal identifiers and confidential client material.

## Analysis rule

Observe how a question changes across years, but never infer sector-wide prevalence from meeting frequency. Distinguish announcements, funded activities, applicant experiences and later outcomes. Identify contradictory source dates and conflicting testimony instead of silently reconciling them.

## Versioning

Every publication update should include a dated change in GitHub history. Machine-readable data must carry source references and denominator or unit. Provisional future signals and proposed measures remain separately tagged.

## Initial source family

P01 and P02 are 2025 author-prepared research briefs. P03 is a related institutional grant submission. A01 is a private 2019 to 2026 source-control inventory. S01 to S06 are public Saudi statistical or institutional publications. The public reference register on sources.html describes each item.

This text describes the standards being applied; it does not claim every historical item is already verified.
