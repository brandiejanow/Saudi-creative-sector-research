Methods

A clear distinction between what the sources show and what the research concludes.

Publication standards for the ongoing archive review.

Source review

Evidence before interpretation.

The historical archive includes different kinds of material collected for different purposes. The review will identify each item's date, origin, context, research relevance and publication permissions before it is used to support a public finding.

How claims are classified

01

Documented

A statement that can be traced to a dated primary record or suitable public source, with its limits made clear.

02

Reported

What a participant or source said. The record supports the account, not necessarily its universal accuracy.

03

Observed

An experience or event witnessed by the researcher, with dates and setting recorded where known.

04

Interpretation

The author's analysis, identified separately from quotations, external statistics or direct observation.

05

Unverified

A question or statement without adequate support. It is not presented as an established research finding.

Research independence

Authorship and permissions matter.

Original independent research is kept distinct from outside benchmarks, organizational records and research commissioned by others. Confidential records, identifiable participant material and research belonging to another organization are not published without the necessary rights or permissions.

Neither professional reputation nor institutional status substitutes for evidence. Public comparisons will consider sources, research methods and the extent to which a claim can be supported.

Saudi Creative Sector Research is independent research begun in 2019 by Brandie Janow. This public record is maintained on GitHub, with source review and publication continuing.
