Design and Saudi Arabia’s
Creative Economy

Independent research   /   Saudi Arabia   /   2019–2026

Abstract

This research examines how creative activity is commissioned, developed, produced, financed, and used in Saudi Arabia. It considers relationships among demand, production capacity, institutional decisions, and the distribution of resources and rights. The inquiry follows how knowledge, specialist services, and organizational arrangements connect these activities over time.

Qualitative accounts and project documentation are read alongside published statistics and policy materials. The analysis asks how expertise is recognized and retained, who participates in decisions, and how opportunities differ across regions. It also develops questions about cultural authorship, public use, and material consequences.

Development of the inquiry

  1. 2019

    Design education and local capability

    Early research on how design was understood, taught, and supported, including production capacity and the transfer of specialist knowledge.

  2. 2020

    From concept to production

    Manufacturing, supplier access, contracts, distribution, and conditions affecting the development and circulation of creative products and services.

  3. 2021

    Skills, professions, and mentorship

    Links between education and employment, emerging specialist roles, experience, and access to professional guidance.

  4. 2022

    Shared resources and professional services

    Membership models, mentorship matching, service provision, access costs, and the resources required to sustain collective support.

  5. 2023

    Professional value and client relationships

    Qualitative research addressing education, career access, client expectations, and how design expertise was understood and valued.

  6. 2024

    Technical capacity and research development

    Shared facilities, specialist services, professional support, and methods for examining the sector more systematically.

  7. 2025

    Financing and geographic access

    Authored studies of eligibility, licensing, private investment, production support, overlooked occupations, and differences across regions.

  8. 2026

    Contracts, collaboration, and measurement

    Research into procurement, payments, mentoring, and professional continuity, alongside creative-index questions and comparison with published indicators.
