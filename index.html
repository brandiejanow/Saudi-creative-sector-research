Academic Research

Saudi Arabia’s Creative Economy

Brandie Janow   /   2019–2026

Abstract

This research examines the conditions that enable or constrain the development of Saudi Arabia’s creative economy, with an emphasis on design. Central concerns include how expertise and production resources become accessible, how funding bodies, commissioning organizations, and markets allocate opportunities, and how knowledge, rights, and value are retained over time.

Original qualitative material collected since 2019 provides the primary source base, including facilitated focus groups and documented accounts of professional and organizational experience. Selected transcripts were thematically coded using documented codebooks. Authored notes and project records provide additional context. Published statistics, policy materials, and international comparisons supply contextual and comparative benchmarks.

Timeline

  1. 2019

    Demand and local capability

    Talent alone does not explain sector development; demand, production, and knowledge transfer need to be considered together.

  2. 2020

    From concept to production

    Manufacturing, contracts, and distribution affect whether ideas can become viable products and services.

  3. 2021

    Skills and experience

    Career pathways need to connect specialist skills and experience with education, mentorship, and opportunities.

  4. 2022

    Shared resources

    Shared resources need sustainable service models, clear access arrangements, and continued support between projects.

  5. 2023

    Recognition and access

    Participant accounts linked undervaluation with gaps in client understanding and uneven access to networks and mentorship.

  6. 2024

    Design across disciplines

    Knowledge of Design launched with material ecology, language, and cross-disciplinary learning among its subjects, alongside continuing research on education and local capability.

  7. 2025

    Conditions for access

    Funding is one part of access: legal status, technical resources, regional reach, and support for experimentation also matter.

  8. 2026

    Experimentation and cultural continuity

    Agency, experimentation, and material capacity were examined together, with attention to how design is used, discussed, and carried forward.
