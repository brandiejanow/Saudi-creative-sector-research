Saudi Creative
Sector Research

A longitudinal examination of Saudi Arabia’s creative economy, with an emphasis on design. The research traces professional roles, local production, education and employment, commercial relationships, financing, and the exchange of expertise as the sector developed.

Independent research   /   Saudi Arabia   /   2019–2026

Abstract

Across the historical record, questions about creative careers extend well beyond the availability of cultural programs. They include how graduates and self-taught professionals entered emerging occupations; why specialist, academic, and research roles could be overlooked; how designers accessed suppliers, prototyping and fabrication; and how clients understood, commissioned, priced, and paid for creative services.

The inquiry also follows access to public and private finance, regional differences across the Kingdom, the contribution of international expertise, and the prospects for Saudi professionals to establish lasting careers. Dated qualitative materials are examined alongside authored study briefs and published statistics. The national surveys and service maps proposed in 2025 are distinguished from research already undertaken, so the public record does not mistake an ambition for a result.
