About the research

What has changed, and for whom?

This research examines how Saudi Arabia's design professions and creative sector have developed, including the institutions, policies, businesses and people involved. It considers changes in education, access to opportunities, professional conditions, commissioning, funding and production.

The purpose is to establish what the historical record can support. A reported change is not automatically evidence that the same change occurred across the entire sector.
