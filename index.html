Academic Research

Saudi Arabia’s Creative Economy

Brandie Janow   /   2019–2026

Abstract

This research examines the conditions that enable or constrain the development of Saudi Arabia’s creative economy, with an emphasis on design. Central concerns include how expertise and production resources become accessible, how funding bodies, commissioning organizations, and markets allocate opportunities, and how knowledge, rights, and value are retained over time.

Original qualitative material collected since 2019 provides the primary source base, including facilitated focus groups and documented accounts of professional and organizational experience. Selected transcripts were thematically coded using documented codebooks. Authored notes and project records provide additional context. Published statistics, policy materials, and international comparisons supply contextual and comparative benchmarks.

Timeline

  1. 2019

    Design education and local capability

    Early research on how design was understood, taught, and supported, including production capacity and the transfer of specialist knowledge.

  2. 2020

    From concept to production

    Manufacturing, supplier access, contracts, distribution, and conditions affecting the development and circulation of creative products and services.

  3. 2021

    Skills, professions, and mentorship

    Links between education and employment, emerging specialist roles, experience, and access to professional guidance.

  4. 2022

    Shared resources and professional services

    Membership models, mentorship matching, service provision, access costs, and the resources required to sustain collective support.

  5. 2023

    Qualitative research on market access

    Facilitated group research examined client understanding, education, professional networks, mentorship, and access to resources. The findings informed Saudi Creative Sector Insights.

  6. 2024

    Qualitative research and first publications

    Saudi Creative Sector Insights and Saudi Vision were first published in 2024. The January group discussion addressed education, procurement, local capability, professional recognition, and environmental responsibility.

  7. 2025

    Community profiles and research briefs

    The June 2025 Community Overview documented career, industry, and geographic profiles. Authored briefs developed questions about financing, production support, overlooked occupations, and regional access.

  8. 2026

    Published data and related design research

    The Community Overview published in May 2026 presented career and interest profiles alongside updated qualitative reports. Making a Design Culture and the KoD Compendium added conceptual and program syntheses.
