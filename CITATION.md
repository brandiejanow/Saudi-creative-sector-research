# How to cite this research

**Research began in 2019. The public website was established in 2026.** These are different dates.

For the evolving website:

Janow, B. (2026). *Saudi Creative Sector Research: Independent research begun in 2019* [Research website and evolving source review]. https://brandiejanow.github.io/Saudi-creative-sector-research/

For the first research dossier:

Janow, B. (2026). *Financial Infrastructure and Creative Careers: Source commentary on 2025 research proposals* [Working paper 01]. Saudi Creative Sector Research. https://brandiejanow.github.io/Saudi-creative-sector-research/funding.html

For a particular section, add its direct URL and access date. No DOI has been issued. The dossier has not been presented as a peer-reviewed empirical journal article.

The original 2025 proposals are unpublished archival documents. Direct quotations from other people's recorded speech have not yet been cleared for publication.

Public statistics should be cited to the original organizations in [Sources & Notes](sources.html), not attributed as data collected by this project.
