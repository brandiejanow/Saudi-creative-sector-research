Sources & Notes

A register of the documents behind this public edition, including the limits of what each reference can support.

Two related 2025 study designs   |   One confidential original research corpus   |   Six public institutional sources

01 / Reference standard

Sources and interpretation limits

Sources are assigned identifiers so a claim in the text can be traced to the item behind it. Privately held sources are described without publishing participant details, confidential files or internal storage links.

Some government documents identify official activity, some provide statistical measures, and the 2025 research drafts identify historical research questions. They should not be presented as interchangeable forms of proof.

03 / Source questions

Known issues still requiring review.

Dates: a report and related private 2023 research records have an unresolved date difference. The discrepancy is retained privately instead of publishing an unsupported date.

Original authorship: private original source details remain in the restricted archive. Research analysis does not publish identifiable individuals or confidential records, and publication permissions remain distinct from research access.

Evidence coverage: the number of archived files is not a count of unique research participants or completed national studies.

Outcome claims: proposed studies, announced financing routes, and actual supported projects are distinct. Each has its own source and evidentiary limits.

Data comparison: cultural participation figures from different years will not be shown as a measured trend until the survey definitions can be compared.
