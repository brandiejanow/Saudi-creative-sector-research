Working study 01 / Private original research / 2019 to 2026

Unseen
Infrastructures

Examining the professional, technical, legal, financial and regional systems behind Saudi Arabia's design industry and creative sector.

Historical research: 2019 to 2026   |   Study framework: 2025

Abstract

What makes creative practice possible?

The study examines the conditions required to enter a creative profession, find clients, access production facilities and professional services, establish prices and continue earning a livelihood. Cultural events and institution counts do not, by themselves, explain these conditions.

Original qualitative research gathered from 2019 onward provides the historical basis. In 2025, the research questions were organized into a formal study design examining what the working document called the unseen infrastructure behind creative activity. Its extended version contained fourteen questions and a proposed national research methodology. The different versions of this document are not fourteen separate studies, nor does the 2025 study design replace the private research already conducted.

The primary evidence is maintained in a restricted research archive. Public analysis will use anonymized source coding, without identifying people, organizations or individual source sessions. Source dates and full context remain privately recorded for verification.

Research problem

Services behind public cultural activity.

The 2025 study design examined questions around legal and business advice, production and technical access, employment classification, education, mentorship, pricing, geographic inequality and the continuity of professional careers. [P04]

These subjects arose through earlier research, but they cannot all be treated as nationwide rates or conditions. Each research claim must distinguish a participant's experience, an observed practice, a proposed response, and evidence that an outcome changed.

Existing evidence

Qualitative evidence and longitudinal analysis

The 2019 to 2026 archive contains research material gathered through professional inquiry, first-hand observation and accounts of sector conditions. Relevant materials are coded as original qualitative evidence. Names and identifying details are not part of the public presentation.

The public study will present recurring topics, supporting context, differences of perspective and changes over time. Original records remain securely retained for verification. The number of files should not be mistaken for the number of unique participants or a representative statistical sample.

Further research designed in 2025

Nationwide investigation was a proposed next stage.

The extended study proposed research tools, a landscape survey across thirteen regions, further interviews and focus groups, infrastructure mapping and contextual comparison. It proposed a national report, a regional map, an occupational typology and a structural barriers index.

These later stages must be verified separately before being described as completed. That distinction does not reduce the research already gathered from 2019 onward, which is the historical foundation of the study.

Related inquiry

Financial infrastructure and access.

A second study design, developed alongside the infrastructure proposal, examined funding and long-term financial conditions. It contained eighteen numbered research questions. Both grew from the same historical inquiry but address different subjects. [P05]
