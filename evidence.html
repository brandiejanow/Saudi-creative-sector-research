Evidence / Source register / Public statistics

Evidence & Data

Public figures are necessary for context, but their scope, population, and collection method determine what they can establish.

Source references: S01 to S06   |   Original archival material: P01 to P03 / A01

01 / Reading the data

Measures that answer different questions.

Official statistics, institutional announcements, interviews, meeting records, and grant proposals answer different kinds of questions. This page records numbers from identified public sources, rather than adding figures from inaccessible or unfinished parts of the archive.

The numbers below describe different populations and program periods. No single figure proves the overall condition of the Saudi design industry or whether independent practitioners can find opportunities, receive payment, or remain professionally active.

02 / Comparability

Do not interpret two percentages as a trend without checking their definitions.

The 2024 household survey reported 81.6% for visits to cultural events or activities among its defined survey population. A later announcement reported 75.3% for 2025. Before treating the difference as a change in behavior, the full questionnaires, weighting, reference period, eligibility, and categories must be assessed.

Similarly, the Cultural Development Fund's cumulative support and the count of funded projects are not the same as a probability that an applicant can obtain funding. An application denominator and the selection criteria are required before making that claim.

03 / What institutional figures do not show

Access, experience, and continuity require their own measures.

The number of event days does not reveal who could participate or whether participation produced lasting professional opportunities. The number of facilities does not establish whether the facilities were reachable, affordable, or useful to particular communities.

A report of total funding does not establish whether independent designers, first-time applicants, rural businesses, or informal cultural organizers benefited. Those questions require more detailed administrative data and properly designed qualitative research.

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Culture has itself described classification difficulties in cultural employment statistics, including the difference between cultural occupations inside and outside cultural institutions. The 2024 State of Culture report identifies limits in available labor data. [S06]

04 / Evidence classification

The project does not merge unlike sources.

Primary archival document: establishes what was recorded, proposed, decided, or reported in a particular discussion. It does not automatically prove that a later action took place.

Author observation: a dated account of something witnessed or experienced. Wider conclusions need independent corroboration.

Participant account: evidence of that participant's perspective within the session's scope. It does not represent the whole Saudi population.

Public official statistic: describes the defined population, program, or indicator reported by its publisher, subject to its stated limits.

Working interpretation: an argument based on specified sources, separated from the underlying evidence.

Future signal: a question about a possible change. It remains an analytical proposition until independently supported.

05 / Open research needs

Evidence still needed for the core questions.

Access to financial support: program eligibility and applications by discipline, legal status, business size, region and outcome.

Professional continuity: follow-up evidence on repeat commissions, payment, operating costs, retention and closure.

Manufacturing and technical access: verified supplier availability, prototyping costs, production quality, and service locations at particular dates.

Creative labor classification: which kinds of occupations and workers are included in official counts and which are outside the measured categories.

Time comparison: consistent questions, comparable sources, and dated outcomes across years, not simply differences in the number of archived files.
