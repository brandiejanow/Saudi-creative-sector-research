02 / Comparability

Comparability of participation measures

The 2024 household survey reported 81.6% for its defined cultural participation measure, while a later announcement reported 75.3% for 2025. Comparison requires evidence that the questions, reference periods, and sampling procedures are compatible.

03 / What institutional figures do not show

Limits of administrative indicators

Event days, facilities, and workforce counts indicate program scale or employment under a particular definition. They do not independently measure professional income, regional access, or subsequent commissions.

The Ministry of Culture's 2024 State of Culture report also describes difficulties in classifying cultural employment, including occupations within and outside cultural institutions. Ministry of Culture, 2024
