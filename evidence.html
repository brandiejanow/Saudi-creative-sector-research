Evidence & Data

What can be documented, what has been reported, and what still requires verification.

The public evidence catalogue is being assembled from the original archive.

Types of evidence

Not every source proves the same thing.

01

Original research

Dated interviews, meeting notes, transcripts, research discussions and other records collected directly as part of the research. Conclusions must reflect the participants and circumstances involved.

02

Historical documents

Contemporary reports, policies, project briefs, programme records and other documents that can establish what was recorded at a particular time.

03

Firsthand observations

The author's observations, identified by date, place and context where available. These help document an experience but do not alone establish conditions across a population.

04

Outside research

Independent studies and public statistics used for comparison, with the original authors and methods credited. They are not relabeled as this project's original findings.

Public records

Publication follows verification.

Datasets and supporting records will be added only when they can be responsibly released. Future entries will identify the source, date, scope, collection method, status and limits of the evidence.

This page does not imply that the research is representative of all people or businesses in Saudi Arabia. Any claim about wider populations will require an appropriate sample or another suitable basis.

Saudi Creative Sector Research is independent research begun in 2019 by Brandie Janow. This public record is maintained on GitHub, with source review and publication continuing.
