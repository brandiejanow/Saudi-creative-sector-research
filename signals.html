Research agenda / Futures / Unverified propositions

Future Signals

A register of possible changes suggested by earlier questions and public developments, with the evidence required to test each one.

Signals are not predictions, statistics, or completed findings.

01 / Purpose

Purpose and status

The signals below are research propositions. They help decide what evidence to look for next and what changes might be important. They are not statements that a future outcome is likely or inevitable.

Each item keeps an origin period, proposed question, evidence requirement and review status. Forecasts, fabricated scores, and arbitrary confidence percentages are intentionally excluded.

02 / Working register

Questions to test across future periods.

FS01   |   2021 working sources

New professions and missing entry routes

Research question: If new occupations become visible through market demand before education or licensing pathways are established, how do people enter them?

What must be checked: Verify training provision, occupation standards, recruitment records, and dated practitioner accounts.

Status: Question under review. Source notes are in the private archive or public records listed in Sources & Notes.

FS02   |   2022 working sources; 2025 grant brief

Funding systems and their access costs

Research question: Could application requirements, financial preparation, and service fees exclude some of the people a support program is meant to reach?

What must be checked: Compare eligibility criteria, application completion, pricing, applicant types, and declines.

Status: Question under review. Source notes are in the private archive or public records listed in Sources & Notes.

FS03   |   2025 authored drafts

Continuity after the first opportunity

Research question: Do first commissions, grants and short programmes lead to continuing income, repeat clients, and sustainable operating arrangements?

What must be checked: Longitudinal follow-up, contracting and payment records, and repeat engagement data.

Status: Question under review. Source notes are in the private archive or public records listed in Sources & Notes.

FS04   |   2019 to 2021 source index

Local production and supplier information

Research question: Does better visibility of suppliers and facilities reduce barriers to making and delivering design locally?

What must be checked: Supplier mapping, price comparison, production capacity and user outcomes.

Status: Question under review. Source notes are in the private archive or public records listed in Sources & Notes.

FS05   |   2024 to 2026 archival context; public ministry report

Measurement and professional classification

Research question: How might measurement systems miss people whose creative occupation or contribution falls outside the categories used by a survey?

What must be checked: Review GASTAT definitions, labor categories, establishment surveys and primary interviews.

Status: Methodological question. Source notes are in the private archive or public records listed in Sources & Notes.

FS06   |   2024 financing launch; 2025 proposals; 2026 fund reporting

The distance between investment and access

Research question: Can reported financial expansion coexist with persistent barriers for particular applicants, disciplines, or regions?

What must be checked: Application denominators, rejected applications, geographical records and follow-up outcomes.

Status: Comparative question. Source notes are in the private archive or public records listed in Sources & Notes.

03 / Review rule

Repetition does not automatically establish change.

An observation becoming common in professional discussion may reflect wider conditions, a change in who is speaking, or a rise in public attention. The publication will not treat frequency in social media or a small self-selected group as a population trend.

A future study could test these questions with repeated sampling and comparable measures, while retaining interviews and field accounts to explain what a numerical shift means.
