Future Signals

Possible changes suggested by earlier research and reported developments, with evidence needed to test each proposition.

Signals are not predictions, statistics, or completed findings.

01 / Purpose

Purpose and status

The six questions identify possible changes for future investigation. Each specifies a record period and the evidence needed for assessment.

02 / Propositions

Research propositions

FS01   |   2021 working sources

New professions and missing entry routes

Question: If new occupations become visible through market demand before education or licensing pathways are established, how do people enter them?

Evidence required: Verify training provision, occupation standards, recruitment records, and dated practitioner accounts.

FS02   |   2022 working sources; 2025 grant brief

Funding systems and their access costs

Question: Could application requirements, financial preparation, and service fees exclude some of the people a support program is meant to reach?

Evidence required: Compare eligibility criteria, application completion, pricing, applicant types, and declines.

FS03   |   2025 authored drafts

Continuity after the first opportunity

Question: Do first commissions, grants and short programs lead to continuing income, repeat clients, and sustainable operating arrangements?

Evidence required: Longitudinal follow-up, contracting and payment records, and repeat engagement data.

FS04   |   2019 to 2021 source index

Local production and supplier information

Question: Does better visibility of suppliers and facilities reduce barriers to making and delivering design locally?

Evidence required: Supplier mapping, price comparison, production capacity and user outcomes.

FS05   |   2024 to 2026 archival context; Ministry of Culture report

Measurement and professional classification

Question: How might measurement systems miss people whose creative occupation or contribution falls outside the categories used by a survey?

Evidence required: Review GASTAT definitions, labor categories, establishment surveys and primary interviews.

FS06   |   2024 financing launch; 2025 proposals; 2026 fund reporting

The distance between investment and access

Question: Can reported financial expansion coexist with persistent barriers for particular applicants, disciplines, or regions?

Evidence required: Application denominators, rejected applications, geographical records and follow-up outcomes.
