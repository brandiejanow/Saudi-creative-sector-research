# Public data dictionary

These CSV files are separate supporting registers and do not form a single national research dataset.

## public_indicators.csv

Each row includes an indicator ID, topic, reference period, reported value, unit, description, source ID, official source URL, and interpretation limit. Source indicators do not describe the same populations or programs. No comparison should be made without checking definitions and denominators.

## research_questions.csv

Thirty-two research questions organized across two 2025 study designs: fourteen infrastructure questions and eighteen financial questions. They are research questions, not conclusions, interview results, or evidence that proposed studies were completed.

## future_signals.csv

Questions about possible change, with the origin period and evidence required. These are not forecasts or statistical findings.

## Exclusions

The historical archive includes private original qualitative research and some other materials classified outside this study. It is not being published as a public participant dataset. No raw qualitative sources, private participant identities, confidential client files or separately controlled institutional research are included here.

New or corrected figures must retain their original source and dates in GitHub history.
